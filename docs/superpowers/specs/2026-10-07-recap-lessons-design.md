# Recap lessons — design (approved 2026-10-07)

## What the owner asked for
One recap lesson per subject. The top hat in a couple of settings that fit the subject
(philosophy: ancient Greece; science: a lab) but are new places, not the lessons' own scenes.
It opens slowly (him busy, sound effects, small sounds), then recaps ACCURATELY what the
road's lessons taught, in a fun way. The art should be fuller and more realistic than the
lessons: real rooms and real outdoors, using more of the screen, gamified, smooth.

## Decisions (owner's picks)
- **Settings match the subject**, new places with the subject's feel.
- **Cast: the top hat plus one other** cast member, chosen per subject. A conversation, as
  every lesson is (group AP): the other half-remembers, mixes things up or brags; the top
  hat sets it straight (group AS).
- **Three questions**, each a different small game on the stage (group AU), each drawing on
  two of the six lessons.
- **Backgrounds: recaps first.** The fuller backgrounds are built in the recaps; the 42
  lessons get them later, after the owner approves the look.
- **One recap first (philosophy)** for the owner to see, then the other six.

## Shape
- **Where:** stop 7 on each road, `<subject>-foundations-7`, after lesson 6. Its road sign
  carries a RECAP mark. Lessons added later go after it.
- **Slow open:** beat 0 waits (`voiceAfter`, about 6 s) while the top hat works, with
  sound cues; then a short murmur of his own. Then the second character arrives.
- **The recap:** about 12–14 voiced lines across two or three settings, linked by a walk or
  a camera move. Each of the six lessons gets at least one line restating its key idea, and
  every claim is taken from what that lesson says (its lines, quizzes and summary).
- **Close:** a quote and the summary card, as every lesson.

## The look
- **A taller stage:** the scene band is about 400 × 300 instead of 400 × 208, so the
  picture fills the stage box (today about a third of it is empty paper).
- **Full settings, in layers:** far (sky, hills, back wall), middle (buildings, columns,
  shelves), near (floor, bench, props). Drawn against reference photographs in the flat
  editorial style of the redesigned lessons. The still layers are baked to pictures
  (`make-lesson-art`, AM13; plates as in AT7) so the scene stays smooth on a phone.
- **Objects** are drawn pictures that animate (pour, fizz, unroll, swing).

## Holds
All the lesson rules still hold: AP (dialogue), AQ (words fit), AR (real colours, natural
hands), AS (the cast), AT6–AT9 (sound), AU (questions as games), N22 (no unison), K19 (the
speaker on camera), AP17/AP22 (voice, natural style).

## Philosophy recap
- **Cast:** the top hat and the plain one. He was at the centre of four philosophy lessons
  (the bicycle, the stopped clock, the teleporter, the fudge oracle), so he "remembers" them,
  wrongly and grandly.
- **Settings:** the Athenian agora in the morning (a stoa colonnade, a fountain house, market
  stalls, the Acropolis on its hill behind), then down to the harbour (a Greek ship whose
  planks have all been replaced, which is the bicycle puzzle of lesson 1, and a sundial).
