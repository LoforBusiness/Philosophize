Originals were PNG screenshots totalling 5.87 MB; resized to a 1080px maximum and
re-encoded as quality-90 JPEG (1.40 MB total) so they do not weigh down every OTA
update. Re-encoding shifted measured luminance by less than 0.003, so the tone
assignments in data/profileBackgrounds.ts are unaffected.

After adding or replacing any file here, run:

    node scripts/check-profile-contrast.mjs

It measures each image and fails if the text on it would drop below WCAG AA, or if
the tone declared in data/profileBackgrounds.ts disagrees with the measurement.
